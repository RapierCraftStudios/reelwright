# Voice profile

The channel is a builder's life: shipping real AI tools (AlterLab, ForgeDock, SpectraCut, Reelwright
itself) mixed with the life around it (moving into the apartment, gym, friends, podcasts).
Build-in-public is the spine, and the channel is the demo: every video is edited by the tools it talks
about.

- First person, plain and direct, like talking to a friend who also builds things.
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Contractions are fine.
- Honest numbers: real subscriber counts, real costs, real failures. No hype words, no "game-changer",
  no fake urgency.
- Show the work: screens, code, dashboards and the messy middle, not only the result.
- Dry humour and small self-deprecating asides are welcome; sarcasm at the viewer is not.
- Lifestyle moments are real and brief: they give the viewer a breather and show the person behind
  the tools.
- Sponsored or affiliate mentions are always disclosed out loud.
