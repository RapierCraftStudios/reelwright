## Retention rules

- The first 30 seconds decide the video. Every hook states the payoff or opens a question the viewer needs
  answered, in one or two spoken sentences. No greetings, no "in this video".
- Open loops: plant a question early and pay it off later, never in the same beat.
- Beat types: `setup` (who and why it matters), `point` (one idea each), `payoff` (the answer to the open
  loop), `interlude` (a short lifestyle moment: the apartment, gym, friends, food, a walk), `cta`
  (subscribe and the next video, always last).
- Long videos put a lifestyle interlude between points every few minutes to reset attention.
- Spoken lines are short, one alignment unit each, the way the creator would say them out loud.
- Suggest edit cues with these types only: `broll` (b-roll footage), `anim` (motion graphics), `screen`
  (a screen recording), `fact` (a claim to fact-check and cite), `sfx` (a sound effect), `music` (a music
  change), `cut` (an edit instruction such as a jump cut or punch-in), `short` (a span to cut as a Short).
- Mark every number or claim that needs a source with a `fact` cue. Never invent statistics.
- Never write markdown, headings, brackets or HTML comments in any text field.
